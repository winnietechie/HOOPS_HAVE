Our Story

Founded in 2010 by former professional basketball player Michael Johnson, Hoops Haven began with a simple mission: to provide high-quality basketball equipment to players at all levels.

What started as a small store in downtown Chicago has grown into one of the most trusted names in basketball equipment, serving customers across the country.

We test every product we sell to ensure it meets our high standards for performance and durability. Our team consists of former players, coaches, and basketball enthusiasts who are passionate about the game.
